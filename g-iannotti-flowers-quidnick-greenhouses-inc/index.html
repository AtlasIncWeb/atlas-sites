How delivery works

Every bouquet is made in house and delivered by the shop, so the flowers that arrive are the flowers that were arranged here.

  1. Order before 1:00 p.m. for same-day delivery. Orders after that go out the following day.
  2. A $10.00 local delivery fee is added to each order, for each address. Outside the local delivery area, that fee may vary.
  3. No Sunday deliveries. Anything requested for a Sunday is delivered the next business day.
  4. Around the holidays, place the order at least one day ahead to help assure it arrives on time.
  5. Sending overseas? Call the shop directly — international orders cannot be taken over the internet.
