Three posts we wrote for you

Seasonal tip · Facebook

Late July storms in Warwick arrive fast. Next time it pours, step outside and watch your gutters. Water sheeting over the front edge instead of running down the downspout means a clog, not heavy rain.

Siding tip · Facebook

July humidity is why the shaded side of a house turns green first. That film on vinyl siding is mildew, not dirt, and a careful wash lifts it off. Scrubbing hard only scars the panel.

Post idea · Instagram

A dry stretch in high summer is the best window for a roof replacement in Rhode Island. Shingles seal down properly in the heat, and there is no November cold snap to race.
