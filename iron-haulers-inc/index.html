About Iron Haulers

Established in 2005, Iron Haulers Inc. is a veteran-owned, small business founded by Denzil "Denny" Easton. Denny is a United States Marine Corps veteran with extensive experience in the construction industry.

He has worked in the industry since 1997, gaining experience in the mechanical operations of equipment, as well as appraising, renting, selling and transporting heavy construction equipment.

"Our employees are some of the best in the industry and all ensure professionalism, safety, timeliness and security of your property." Iron Haulers, in their own words
