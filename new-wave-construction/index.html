What we take on

A general contractor is the person who owns the whole outcome — the sequence, the trades, the schedule and the mess. These are the three kinds of building we do it on.

01

Residential

Houses, and the particular problem of working inside a building that somebody is still living in. Water off for an afternoon, dust where the kids are, a kitchen that has to come back tonight — a residential job gets judged on how it was run at least as much as on how it came out.

02

Commercial

Buildings that have to open again. On commercial work the schedule is part of the budget: every week a space is not in use is part of what the job actually cost, which is why sequencing the trades matters more than anything else on site.

03

Restoration

Bringing an existing building back rather than replacing it. Rhode Island is full of buildings worth keeping, and restoration is the work of making one of them usable again without erasing what it already was.
