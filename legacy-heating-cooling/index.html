Four guarantees, not four slogans

Most contractors promise quality. Legacy publishes four specific guarantees and names what each one covers. These are theirs, summarized from their own words.

01

The No Surprise Guarantee

No surprise pricing: the quote you get up front is the price you'll be paying — no hidden fees, no shocking number at the end of the job. Any additional work is approved by the owner, in writing, before it starts.

02

We Stand by Our Work 100%

Equipment they install will perform as stated, or they stand behind it with warranties, guarantees and follow-up service. Issues inside the warranty period are addressed at no extra cost to the customer.

03

Peace of Mind Guarantee

Every technician working in your home or business is highly trained and has had an extensive background check performed for your safety.

04

Property Guarantee

Lawns, shrubbery, carpeting, floors, walls, furniture and door frames are protected throughout an installation. Damaged property is replaced or repaired at their cost.

If your system kicks on and off every few minutes, that is called short cycling. It usually points to airflow or a sizing issue, not a system that needs replacing. We can tell you which one you have.

Plenty of older East Greenwich homes were never built with ductwork. Ductless systems mount high on the wall and handle one room at a time, no ducts required. Ask us what would fit yours.

maintenance tip · Facebook

A clogged filter makes your system work harder than it needs to for the same result. Checking it is a two minute job you can do yourself, and it is one of the easiest ways to make equipment last.
