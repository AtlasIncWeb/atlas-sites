What you train here

In their own words: “At Armor of God Dojo, we offer top-notch training in Boxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ), MMA, Muay Thai, and Wrestling.” Classes are held mostly on weekday evenings.

Boxing

Hands, footwork and the discipline of taking a shot without losing your head.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Grappling on the ground, learned slowly, with partners who watch out for you.

Muay Thai

Elbows, knees and clinch work — the striking art that uses everything.

MMA

Where the striking and the grappling stop being separate subjects.

Wrestling

Getting the takedown, and getting back up. The base underneath the rest.

Not a gym. A dojo.

Walking into a new room is the hard part. Their answer to that is on their own website, and it is worth reading before you call.

“We understand that stepping into a new gym can be intimidating, but this isn’t a gym, it’s a dojo; and I’m not a random pro athlete, I’m a real teacher and lifelong coach.” Armor of God Dojo, in their own words

They say you will be training with a multiple time world champion coach with an intense, rewarded and extensive teaching background — someone who has been going to seminars on how people think, learn and feel since childhood.

And the students do the rest: “Your fellow students are always happy to partner up the new guys, they are loving people who will actually care about your safety and learning experience.”

Training with a purpose

This is a Christian dojo, and it is direct about why that changes what happens on the mats. It is not only about physical fitness.

  • Building the courage to stand up for yourself and the people you love.
  • Friendships that are actually worth something, rooted in friendship and love.
  • A structured outlet for anger, anxiety and depression, and a way to manage stress.
  • A routine you can be proud of — “even if it’s just one hour a week.”
  • A room where faith is a normal thing to talk about, with people who train like you do.

Their motto, “Live like a knight and pray like a knight,” is how they describe the whole thing: living with a sense of purpose, ready to answer the call for valor and truth, and the kind of calm, protective love that keeps a community safe.
