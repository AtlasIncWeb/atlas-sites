The happiest week of somebody's life, the worst one, and all the ordinary days in between. We arrange for all three, and the flowers are chosen for the occasion, not pulled off a shelf.

Bouquets, centerpieces, and everything that has to match on the day. Twenty centerpieces or one stem — both are a real order here.
