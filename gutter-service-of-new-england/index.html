Seamless gutters are formed on site to fit your roofline exactly, so there are no joints along the run where water can work its way loose. Fewer seams, fewer places to fail. That is the whole idea.

Sometimes the gutter is fine and the wood behind it is not. Fascia and soffit carry the whole system, so we check them before we hang anything new. Doing it in the right order saves you a second visit.
