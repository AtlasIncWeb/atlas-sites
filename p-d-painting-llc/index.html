Four things, four different jobs

What they paint

A painting contractor who also does carpentry repairs and murals is really doing four separate trades. Here they are, separated out.

Interior

Rooms, ceilings, trim and doors. The part of the job people judge from two feet away, which is where precision either shows or does not.

Exterior

Siding, trim and the weather-facing surfaces that a Rhode Island winter works on all season whether anyone is looking or not.

Custom murals

Hand-painted work on a wall, made for the room it is in. Their own site gives murals a page of their own.

Carpentry repairs

Rot, soft trim and the boards that need replacing first. Paint does not fix wood — it just hides it for a season.
